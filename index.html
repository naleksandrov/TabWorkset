Features

Everything you need to manage your tabs

🗂

Named Workspaces

Save all your open tabs as a named workspace and restore them in a new window anytime.

⬡

Tab Groups

Saves and restores your tab group structure — colors and names included.

🏷

Tags & Search

Tag workspaces, filter by tag, and search across names, notes, and tab titles instantly.

⏱

Auto-backups

Automatic snapshots every 5 minutes protect your workspaces from accidental deletion.

↺

Version History

Every rename or edit is recorded. Roll back any workspace to any earlier version.

⌨

Keyboard Shortcuts

Alt+S to save, Alt+F to search, Alt+1–9 to restore — all without touching the mouse.

☁

Cloud Sync Pro

Sync workspaces across your devices via built-in browser account sync, or transfer between any browsers using Export / Import JSON.

◷

Scheduled Restore Pro

Open a workspace automatically on specific days and times — no manual action needed.

⊛

Pinned Workspaces Pro

Pin your most-used workspaces to always appear at the top of the list.
